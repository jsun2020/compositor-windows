Local inference in Compositor for Windows

DepthMini is the model published by Robbie Tilton for Compositor.
The ONNX graph converts the original Core ML weights without retraining.
ONNX SHA-256: 06ab4f88a2190a377f2d726bb6c1bd42552a45a915f0a66c71e9aa7feef019bd
Source model: https://github.com/robbietilton/Compositor/releases/tag/depth-model-1
Source and conversion: https://github.com/jsun2020/compositor-windows (scripts/convert-depthmini.py)
DepthMini was distilled from Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache License 2.0) and
initialized from torchvision MobileNetV3 ImageNet weights (BSD 3-Clause).
https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2
https://github.com/pytorch/vision

Foreground segmentation uses U²-Net (Qin et al., 2020), the lightweight U2NETP
model provided in ONNX format by rembg. Licensed under Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/releases/download/v0.0.0/u2netp.onnx
SHA-256: 309c8469258dda742793dce0ebea8e6dd393174f89934733ecc8b14c76f4ddd8
