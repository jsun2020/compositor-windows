# Compositor for Windows 0.13.0

Based on [Mac Compositor 1.6.15](https://github.com/robbietilton/Compositor/commit/a48247eb668e4c00a7c8dafef544758c3012a719).

- Export a layered PSD for Photoshop or After Effects. Groups, layer positions, visibility, opacity, clipping and masks survive export. Live text, shapes and effects become pixel layers. Editable adjustments contribute to the flattened preview. Documents with deep layers export at 16 bits per channel.
- Export selected layers or folders as individually named PNG files trimmed to visible pixels.
- Reduce luminance and color noise in Camera Raw, as a standalone filter, or as an editable adjustment layer. Projects with a Reduce Noise adjustment use format 21. Shapes with an inside stroke use format 22.
- Add up to three colored rim lights. Place volumetric light on a preview, adjust haze and beams, and extract a grayscale depth layer at the original layer's resolution. Both filters work offline. Windows runs the original DepthMini weights through ONNX Runtime and uses U²-Net or existing transparency for subject outlines. Fine silhouettes can differ from Apple Vision. Lighting is estimated on a grid up to 640 pixels on its longest side and applied to the original samples, including deep layers.
- Duplicate, move, hide or convert a selection of layers together. Hide a clipping base to hide its clipped layers in the canvas and exported image. Edit a live shape's fill and inside stroke, and reuse your last new-canvas dimensions.

## Lighting on the same photos

The comparison shows the two filters at their defaults. Volumetric Light was placed at the same point in each photo. “Mac” is the native Mac 1.6.15 renderer. “Port” is the Windows application code running in Chromium on macOS with its bundled offline models. The two renderers use different subject recognition engines. The clearest visible difference is Chelsea's whiskers and lower cheek, where the port lights more fur. The rim on the fingers is very close in this set. [Full photographs](../media/lighting-1615-comparison.webp) · [Enlarged edges](../media/lighting-1615-detail.webp)
